Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Do we use cookies?

Folio does not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies of any kind. We don't run Google Analytics, ad pixels, or any similar tool.

What we do use

To keep you signed in, Supabase (our authentication provider) stores your login session in your browser's local storage — a similar mechanism to a cookie, but not sent automatically with every request the way cookies are. This is strictly functional: without it, you'd have to sign in again on every visit.

Name / typePurposeDuration
Supabase auth session (local storage)Keeps you signed inUntil you sign out or it expires

Third-party resources

  • Google sets its own cookies on google.com during the sign-in step, governed by Google's own cookie policy — Folio does not control these.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr (CDN) may see your IP address when your browser loads fonts or the Supabase code library, as with any external resource.

Do you need to consent to anything?

Strictly necessary/functional storage — used only to keep you logged in, with no tracking or profiling purpose — is generally exempt from cookie-consent requirements under both GDPR and India's guidance. Since Folio currently sets no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies, no cookie-consent banner is required at this time. If analytics or advertising are added in the future, this policy and the consent requirement will be updated accordingly.

Managing local storage

You can clear this data any time via your browser's settings (usually under Privacy → Site Data). Doing so will simply sign you out.
